02 · Problem

Customers need time to shift their consumption

To move washing, charging or heating to a cheaper hour, customers need at least 12 hours of notice. PPC wants to send that notice before the day-ahead auction clears. A window that turns out to be expensive can lead to a cancellation and make the next offer less credible.

03 · Workflow

From the morning forecast to the auction result

Forecast at 10:00

Estimate the chance of a price at or below €20/MWh, hour by hour, for the next seven days.

Propose for D+1 and D+2

Announce windows that meet the automatic rule. Send more uncertain candidates to an operator.

Check the auction

Run confirmation or cancellation rules when official prices arrive.

Explain

Show the evidence, decision history and estimated impact for each proposal.

04 · Agent loop

Perceive, estimate, decide, act

  1. Perceive. The pipelines record each forecast and the cleared day-ahead prices.
  2. Estimate. The models produce hourly probabilities and P10, P50 and P90 price estimates.
  3. Decide. Rules select windows, with review for uncertain proposals.
  4. Act. The prototype records announcements and outcomes in the calendar and prepares messages and alerts.

05 · Data

Build each forecast from information available at issue time

  • Prices. ENTSO-E Romanian day-ahead prices. For 15-minute market periods, the hourly price is the mean of the four quarters.
  • Weather. Archived Open-Meteo forecast runs at the relevant lead time for four locations in Romania.
  • Grid and calendar. Load forecasts, holidays, weekends and past price indicators.

Training ran from January 2023 to February 2025. Validation covered March to September 2025 and included 35,945 forecast rows. March to October 2026 was reserved for the final test.

07 · Thresholds

Higher confidence means fewer windows

In validation, a window counted as a hit when all its hours cleared at or below €20/MWh. Raising the threshold increased the share of hits and reduced the number of candidates.

  • 0.561% · 862 slots
  • 0.665% · 746 slots
  • 0.771% · 630 slots
  • 0.880% · 502 slots

08 · Architecture

One flow from a new dataset to the operator

Scheduled Python pipelines write forecasts and cleared prices to Parquet. Watchdog evaluates triggers on new rows. The agent runs a checkpointed LangGraph workflow per match, and the operator can inspect and resolve cases in the React app.

NestJS, BullMQ on Redis, Postgres and React.

09 · Agent structure

Trigger, playbook, graph

  • Trigger: when to act, based on dataset conditions.
  • Playbook: what action to take, such as announcing, confirming, cancelling or alerting.
  • Graph: how the agent loads context, decides, analyses, requests approval when needed and records the outcome.

10 · Playbooks

Four ways to act on an event

PlaybookRuleReview
AnnounceAt least three consecutive hours at ≥ 0.8 can qualify as Likely. A candidate at ≥ 0.7 can qualify as Possible.Possible requires approval.
ConfirmThe auction trigger detects prices at or below €0/MWh.Automatic.
CancelAn announced slot overlaps a forecast miss or a qualifying drop in the forecast.Operator approval.
AlertFlag conditions such as degraded input data.Internal alert.

12 · Oversight

Keep the decision trace visible

The operator can inspect the rule, the numbers it used and the steps in each case. Claude can explain the evidence and answer questions through read-only tools. The rule determines the action and the operator handles decisions that require approval. Template explanations are available when Claude is unavailable.

13 · Impact and limits

Estimate the value of shifting consumption

The pilot assumptions are 5,000 customers and up to 11.8 MWh shifted per slot. The app estimates the value from the difference between the slot price and the evening P50 price.

These are scenario estimates. The price model overestimated some cheap hours, and aiming to fill the entire monthly allowance raised the estimated cancellation rate in validation.

14 · What’s next

Connect live feeds and add 15-minute forecasts

The next steps are live ENTSO-E and weather feeds and support for 15-minute market periods. The trigger and playbook design can also be extended to meter anomalies, asset health and trading desk alerts.
